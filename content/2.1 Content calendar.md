# 2. Timeline considerations

The preceding objectives are based upon both stipulations in my contract and considerations drawn from the overall content strategy outlined in this document. Together these objectives constitute the bulk of new content I will be working on this year.

Behind each specific task is a certain amount of logistical time and labor required for organizing, ideating, research, reading, communications, etc. For example, the work of writing an original article involves a significant amount of research and data analysis (e.g., processing interviews, selecting and reading relevant sources, selecting and editing photos, etc.) before the actual organization and writing of the text can begin.

These tasks center on content objectives and do not include my other duties, such as fundraising research, contributor recruitment, institutional outreach, and administration. The time requirements in each of these categories may vary over time. For instance, if we switch to Asana as my recommended [[3.8 Task management|task management program]], the process of onboarding and setting up new workflows there will require an initial time sink but will save time on administration in the long run. Similarly, hiring an additional team member to handle time-intensive tasks like InterDialogue editing and social media management would allow me to redistribute the time I spend on those tasks to other areas.

Based on the wide variety of tasks identified here and the varying amounts of time that each requires, as well as my commitments in other categories, we should discuss how to best allocate my 15 weekly hours of InterPlex work to different tasks. While I believe we can develop an efficient workflow and list of priorities to maximize efficient use of my time, we should also remain open to modifying timeline expectations in line with what is possible at 15 hours per week.
